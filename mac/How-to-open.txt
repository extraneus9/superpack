superpack for Mac — apps made by alva. Free and open source (MIT).

1. Double-click "Install superpack".

2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app ("Apple could not verify…").
   That's because it's free and open source, not sold through Apple. To open it:
     • click Done (not Move to Trash),
     • open System Settings › Privacy & Security,
     • scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to "Install superpack", then confirm.
   You only do this once. The apps it installs open normally after that.

3. Pick your apps and click Install. They go into Applications.

Inside: the player (video and audio), Foldernator (a Finder alternative with status colours and themes),
The Prompterinator (teleprompter that follows your voice) and No More Writing (talk instead of typing —
it also downloads its AI models while installing).

No More Writing asks once for Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring: that's how it hears you,
types for you, and listens for its shortcut. Everything runs on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or newer.
The code: https://github.com/extraneus9/superpack
