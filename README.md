# superpack

Free, open-source apps made by alva — built by hand for my own creative work, and shared with anyone who wants them.
No accounts, no ads, nothing sent anywhere. Every app starts clean and is made to be bent to the way you work.

One installer puts them on your computer: pick all of them or only the ones you want.

### the player
A modern video and audio player that opens anything — MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, FLAC, WAV and more — in a blink.

- Three looks to choose from: Glass, Edit Bay and alva.
- A home page with your recent videos, downloads and files as a moving showreel. Turn each row on or off.
- Autoplay on or off: files open playing, or paused until you press play.
- Window motion: instant, smooth or cinematic.
- Stays ready in the background, so files open almost instantly.
- File details for any file — streams, colour, HDR, chapters — one click to copy.
- Keyboard shortcuts for everything.

> Can become your video & audio player in Windows.
>
> On Mac: Opens any video or audio file from Finder: right-click › Open With › the player.

**Runs on:** Windows · Mac · **Code:** [`the player/`](./the%20player) — its README explains how to run and build it.

### Foldernator
A file explorer for Windows that does what Explorer does — and looks the way you want.

- Status colours on folders, so you see where every project stands at a glance.
- Themes, including a real see-through Liquid Glass — or build your own.
- Choose your folder and icon styles.
- Fluidity: no motion, smooth or liquid.
- Media details for any video or audio file (Ctrl+I).
- Your Quick access pins, Home and This PC, just like Windows.

> Folder colours show in Windows' own Explorer too.
>
> On Mac: Works alongside Finder: same files, same Trash, same tags.

**Runs on:** Windows · Mac · **Code:** [`Foldernator/`](./Foldernator) — its README explains how to run and build it.

### The Prompterinator
A teleprompter that scrolls as you speak. It listens on your PC — nothing goes online.

- Voice follow keeps your place while you talk. Or set a speed and go.
- Mirror the text for a glass prompter, or send it to a second screen.
- Fonts, sizes, colours, margins and the reading band — all adjustable.
- Give every speaker their own colour.
- Countdown, on-screen clock and cues for whoever is reading.
- Opens Word, PDF and Excel scripts.
- Save your setups as templates.

> Voice downloads its speech model (about 460 MB) the first time you turn it on.

**Runs on:** Windows · Mac · **Code:** [`The Prompterinator/`](./The%20Prompterinator) — its README explains how to run and build it.

### No More Writing
Press a key, speak, and your words are typed for you — in any app. It all runs on your PC; nothing is sent anywhere.

- Pick your own shortcut (Ctrl+Alt+W to start).
- It tidies what you said: punctuation, line breaks and lists.
- Speed or accuracy — five levels, from instant to most precise.
- Teach it your words: names, brands and your own expressions.
- Style the recording overlay: theme, colour, size and where it sits.
- Choose your microphone; auto level means you never have to shout.
- Works in several languages.

> Downloads its AI models while it installs (about 5 GB). Fastest with an NVIDIA graphics card.
>
> On Mac: Downloads its AI models while it installs (about 5 GB). Runs on your Mac's own chip; asks once for Microphone and Accessibility.

**Runs on:** Windows · Mac · **Code:** [`No More Writing/`](./No%20More%20Writing) — its README explains how to run and build it.

### bloom
Every notification on your PC arrives in bloom's own style: the icon appears first, the message slides out, and your sound plays.

- 8 designs to choose from, from a glowing pill to an arrow-tipped ribbon.
- Colours: each app's own brand colours, 5 presets, or any colour you pick.
- App icons your way: shape, background and logo size.
- Your own sound: drop any audio file on the window; volume up to 150%.
- Where it appears and how long it stays (or the same as Windows).
- Long messages: grow to fit, or only the essentials.
- One switch hides Windows' own popup for every app, and turning it off puts it all back.

> Windows only. Runs quietly next to the clock.

**Runs on:** Windows · **Code:** [`bloom/`](./bloom) — its README explains how to run and build it.

### FrameHUD
A small overlay that shows how your game is really running: frames per second, frame time and your PC's vitals, right on top of the game.

- FPS, frame time and 1% lows for DirectX, OpenGL and Vulkan games.
- GPU use, temperature, VRAM, power and clock; CPU and RAM.
- Ping to the game's own server, found automatically.
- Pick which stats show, their labels and their order.
- Your font, colours, opacity and outline; one row or a list.
- Any corner of any monitor, or drag it where you want.
- Hotkeys: Ctrl+Shift+F10 show/hide, F11 move, F12 settings.

> Windows only. Counting frames needs admin: tick Start with Windows and it never asks again. GPU stats need an NVIDIA graphics card.

**Runs on:** Windows · **Code:** [`FrameHUD/`](./FrameHUD) — its README explains how to run and build it.
